# D11 — GEO / AEO / AI Search

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Google technical/search requirements and current AI-search documentation.
2. First-party platform/search documentation.
3. Secondary research, clearly labeled.
4. Experimental tactics, clearly labeled and never presented as guaranteed ranking/visibility factors.

## Core checks

- content is accessible/indexable and available in textual form where important;
- entity names, relationships, and authorship are clear;
- direct answers are present where users benefit from answer-first structure;
- claims are supported by appropriate sources;
- important information is internally linked and easy to discover;
- content provides original, non-commodity value;
- structured data matches visible content;
- multimodal content has useful textual context.

## Explicit anti-myth rules

Google says there are no additional technical requirements or special AI-only optimizations required for AI Overviews/AI Mode, and no special schema.org markup is required. Do not invent `llms.txt`, AI-specific schema, hidden prompts, or page farms as mandatory ranking mechanisms. citeturn372255search0turn372255search6

Google's current generative-AI guidance emphasizes people-first, unique, useful content and warns against creating large numbers of pages primarily to manipulate rankings or AI responses.

## Reporting

Separate:

- confirmed technical defects;
- content-quality opportunities;
