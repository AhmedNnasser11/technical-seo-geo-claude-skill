# D08 — Internal Linking & Information Architecture

## Checks

- important URLs are reachable through crawlable links;
- navigation and content links use actual `<a href>` semantics or framework equivalents that render crawlable anchors;
- anchor text describes the destination;
- hierarchy and breadcrumbs reflect information architecture;
- orphan pages are identified from the route/link graph;
- excessive click/crawl depth is investigated relative to site scale and discovery needs;
- faceted navigation does not explode crawl paths without an intentional strategy.

Do not use a fixed "three clicks" rule as an absolute requirement. Evaluate discoverability relative to architecture, importance, and crawl paths.
