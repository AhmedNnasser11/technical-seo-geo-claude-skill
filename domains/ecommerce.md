# D10 — Ecommerce SEO

## Checks

- product and category page uniqueness;
- canonical strategy across variants/facets;
- price, currency, availability, and inventory consistency;
- Product/Offer markup appropriate to the page;
- reviews/ratings only when truthful and applicable;
- internal linking across categories/products;
- Merchant Center/feed consistency when relevant;
- out-of-stock and discontinued product handling;
- localized product URLs and metadata.

Google's current product guidance distinguishes product snippets from merchant listings; use the applicable class rather than blindly adding every property.
