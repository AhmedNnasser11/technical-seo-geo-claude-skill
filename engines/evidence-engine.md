# Evidence Engine Contract

Evidence is categorized as:

- `PROJECT_OBSERVATION`
- `STATIC_EXECUTION`
- `RUNTIME_OBSERVATION`
- `OFFICIAL_SOURCE`
- `FIRST_PARTY_IMPLEMENTATION`
- `REPUTABLE_SECONDARY`
- `EXPERIMENTAL_RESEARCH`

Every material conclusion must point to one or more evidence records. The engine rejects findings whose evidence consists only of model assertions or search snippets.
